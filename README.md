## 🧙 Usage (How to Use photoforge)

Here's a quick walkthrough of the main features:

### Open a photo
- Click the **"Open Image"** button at the top left.
- Browse to any image file (JPG, PNG, BMP, GIF, TIFF, or even RAW from many cameras).
- Alternatively, just **drag‑and‑drop** your image into the window.

### Auto-Enhance
- Click **"Magic Fix"** in the toolbar. photoforge will instantly balance lighting, boost colors, and sharpen details.

### Remove Backgrounds
- Go to the **"Tools"** menu → **"Background Removal"**.
- Simply click **"Remove"** — photoforge uses smart AI to detect and remove the back, leaving you with a transparent background.

### Add Text
- Click the **"T"** icon. Type your message, pick a font, adjust size and angle, drag it anywhere.

### Apply Filters
- From the right panel, browse a library of 30+ one‑tap filters (Vintage, Noir, Vivid, etc.).

### Resize & Crop
- Go to **Tools → Resize** and enter new dimensions (keep the aspect ratio box checked).
- Use the **crop tool** to trim the edges.

### Save Your Work
- Click **"Export"** in the bottom‑right.
- Choose the format (JPG for small size, PNG for lossless, PNG for transparency).
- Pick a destination folder and hit **Save**.

💡 **Tip:** If you ever press an undo, **Ctrl+Z** undoes the last action. **Ctrl+Y** redoes.

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### "Windows protected your PC" / SmartScreen warning
If you see a blue or yellow warning when starting photoforge:
1. Click **"More info"** (on Windows 10) or **"Still run"** (Windows 11).
2. Select **"Run anyway"**.
3. The app will open normally.

You only do this once — after that, Windows remembers your choice.

### The app won't start or crashes at launch
- Make sure you've **extracted** the zip (don't try to run from inside the zip).
- Check that you have at least 1 GB free RAM.
- Re‑download and try again.

### The app is missing icons or looks stretched
- Make sure you're running at 100% scaling: Right‑click desktop > **Display settings** > set scaling to **100%**.

### Your color picker doesn't work
- In some cases, Windows update settings interfere. Close other painting tools and retry.

### Can't open a particular image
- photoforge supports most standard formats. If yours is unusual, try converting it online first (like to JPG) then open.

If none of that solves the problem — go to Support below.
